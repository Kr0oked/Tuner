The app listens through your microphone and shows in real time how close each note is,
with a clean interface and no distractions.

Features:
* Chromatic tuner with automatic note detection
* Precise pitch detection down to the cent
* Adjustable reference pitch (410–480 Hz)
* Optional solfège notation (Do, Ré, Mi…)
* Light and Dark theme
* No advertisements
* No trackers
* No unnecessary permissions

Have fun!
